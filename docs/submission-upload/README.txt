Post Studio: submission materials

What it is
A web app that helps an engineer publish on LinkedIn consistently. Claude drafts posts in the author's own voice from their real work, a humanizer removes the AI tone and scores the text, a safety gate blocks client names, internal numbers and unfilled blanks, and nothing is published without a named person's approval.

Files
1. Post-Studio-Demo-Video.mp4   Demo video, about 1 min 43 s, narrated.
2. Post-Studio-Presentation.pptx  15-slide deck. The narration script is in the speaker notes.
3. Post-Studio-One-Pager.pdf     One-page summary: the problems it solves and the numbers.
